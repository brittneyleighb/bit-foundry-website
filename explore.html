Bit Foundry is a growing collection of learning experiences, developer tools, playful software, and systems experiments. Some are useful. Some are strange. All ask what computing could feel like if we designed closer to the machine.

BIT FOUNDRY / 001

Not everything interesting happens in the application layer.

We build downward: into terminals, memory, operating systems, developer environments, and the interfaces between humans and machines. The goal is not complexity for its own sake. It is to expose what is normally hidden, then make it more understandable, expressive, and enjoyable to work with.
